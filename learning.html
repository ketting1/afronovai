Module 1

Ethical Guidelines and Use of AI

Ethics are the principles that help people decide what is right, fair, and responsible when making decisions and carrying out their work with Artificial Intelligence.

1. Introduction to AI Ethics

AI must be fair, safe, transparent, and respectful of human rights. Systems should promote human wellbeing and allow human staff to verify incidents (e.g., before blocking a user's account for suspicious logins). AI should not replace human judgement in important decisions.

2. Australia’s 8 AI Ethics Principles
  • Human wellbeing: Benefit individuals, society, and environment.
  • Human-centred values: Respect human rights and diversity.
  • Fairness: Inclusive and no unfair discrimination.
  • Privacy & Security: Protect data.
  • Reliability & Safety: Operate as intended.
  • Transparency: People know when AI is used.
  • Contestability: People can challenge AI decisions.
  • Accountability: Humans remain responsible.
3. IBM’s 5 Pillars of Trust

1. Fairness (avoid bias)
2. Explainability (understand decisions)
3. Robustness (reliable performance)
4. Transparency (open about use)
5. Data Privacy (protect personal info).

4. Applications in Education & Retail

Education: AI should support learning, not do the work for students. Teachers must remain responsible for outcomes and privacy.

Retail (e.g., TrendMart): Using AI for customer service requires auditing for bias, protecting data, and keeping human oversight.

5. International Standards (ISO & UNESCO)

ISO/IEC 42001: An international standard helping organizations manage AI responsibly (risk management, governance, and continuous improvement).

UNESCO & OECD: Global frameworks promoting human rights, sustainability, and transparency in AI.

6. Australian Legislation & Summary

Existing laws apply to AI, including the Privacy Act 1988, Copyright Act 1968, and Anti-Discrimination Laws. Currently, most AI guidelines in Australia are voluntary frameworks, meaning organizations must self-regulate and maintain human oversight.
